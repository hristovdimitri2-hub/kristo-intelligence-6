Rail 2 is not "legacy" — it is the path for smart wallets

A production seller in the x402 Foundation channel described the same
case we had: Coinbase Smart Wallet, Binance Web3, passkey signers and
exchange withdrawals cannot sign EIP-3009, so they pay with a plain
transfer and prove it. That is not a fallback — it is the only path
those wallets have.

I checked the code first: Rail 2 already exists (main.py:5401, the
X-Payment-Proof header, _decode_payment_proof, _verify_payment_onchain,
12 confirmations, C1 replay guard, H2 endpoint binding). I verified it
live with a real, unconsumed tx: GET /api/v1/whaleflow with a proof
header returned 200 and 16,263 bytes. The missing piece was the label,
not the rail.

What changed — label and positioning only, no rail logic, no change to
the 12 confirmations:

  /llms.txt            "Rail 2 — legacy self-describing proof (fallback)"
                     -> "Rail 2 — smart wallets and exchange withdrawals"
 402 x402_retry_instructions  "MANUAL fallback: ..." -> "SMART WALLETS
                     AND EXCHANGE WITHDRAWALS ..." + "normal path, not a
                     last resort"
 402 payment_proof    new "for" field naming the four wallet classes
 /api/v1/quickstart   "alternative (legacy, slower)" -> "alternative
                     (smart wallets and exchange withdrawals)"
 /api/agent-manifest  "legacy_proof.amount_usdc" -> "proof.amount_usdc";
                     "Legacy alternative" -> "Smart-wallet alternative"
 /ai-plugin.json      "legacy_headers" -> "alternative_headers"
 README.md            "Rail 2 — manual fallback" -> "Rail 2 — smart
                     wallets and exchange withdrawals"

                     X402_RAIL=legacy env value stays — it is a switch

Verification: 1374 tests pass, 1 skipped (full suite, 425 s); 141
targeted tests over the rail, SEO, connectors and viral distribution
pass. The 12-confirmation wait (~24 s) is unchanged and still stated
everywhere the rail is described.

The "legacy" label is still in internal historical records (docs/audit,
ACTION_BOARD, HANDOVER sections 1-39) — those record what was true at
the time, not what to call the rail now.
