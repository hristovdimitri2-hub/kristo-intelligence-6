Rate-limit the paid rail's expensive path

_RATE_LIMIT_DEFAULTS had nine scopes — admin login, checkout, leads, the
webhooks, the two public feeds — and none for the paid rail. A request that
presents a credential costs local EIP-712 recovery and can reach a facilitator
and a gas-paying broadcast, and nothing bounded it: a caller could present
signed-but-unusable payloads as fast as it could open sockets.

Adds the `x402_settlement` scope (120 requests / 5 min per IP), counted ONLY
when a credential is actually present, never for a plain 402. That is
deliberate: the 402 is free by definition and the whole point of a challenge is
that it is free to ask, so limiting it would punish every discovery crawl. The
limit is generous because a real buyer retries a few times, not hundreds.

Test: test_the_settlement_path_is_bounded_but_a_plain_challenge_is_free covers
both directions — a plain 402 does not spend the budget, and the second
credential-bearing request is refused with 429.
