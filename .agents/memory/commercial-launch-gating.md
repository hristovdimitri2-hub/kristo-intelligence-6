---
name: Commercial launch gating
description: Rules for keeping sales, customer messaging and readiness status aligned with verified commercial capability.
---

Public sales calls to action must follow the commercial launch-readiness gate, not merely a running process or successful preview.

**Why:** A browser-visible app can still lack durable production storage, an approved catalog, signed payment fulfillment, reliable Telegram delivery, or a verified x402 settlement. Selling before these are proven risks taking payment without the promised access.

**How to apply:** Keep the public readiness response fail-closed and keep customer checkout actions unavailable while any required gate is blocked. Treat a human catalog approval and real post-Publish payment/delivery smoke tests as external evidence, not as conditions that startup code may simulate or auto-complete.
