Two measurement services were reading as real customers

The same mistake `own_key` was created to fix, one layer out. Both wallets were
falling through to `external` — the class whose Bulgarian label on the board is
"истински клиент" — and neither is a customer:

    settled_scout             0xa1d1c0cf717402ff3651aa4873ccf96410b01978
                              3 payments to us, all delivered, first 2026-10-14
    lumiere_paycheck_tester   0xec2abd3eda89bed90124736e317e847d5fb6d034
                              5 payments to us, first 2026-10-17

They pay us ON PURPOSE to find out whether we deliver. The payment is the
instrument, not the demand, and counting them as customers inflates the customer
base both on the board and in the public classifier — which is precisely the
dishonesty that classifier exists to prevent.

Neither address is a guess. Both are published by their owners:

  settled.tools/llms.txt: "The scout identifies itself (user agent
  `SettledScout`, paying from 0xA1d1C0cf717402ff3651aA4873CcF96410B01978)"

  lumierepaycheck.org/v1/seller-test names 0xec2aBD3E…6d034 as its payTo in its
  own 402 — which is how we read it, and Lumiere's maintainer confirmed on
  issue #45 that the $0.01 at 2026-11-11T23:06:38Z was their test.

Class `canary`: a third party verifying us with a real payment. They stay in the
totals as real settlements — the money did move on chain — they just stop being
counted as demand. `reclassify_known_payers()` runs at boot, so the existing
rows are corrected by this deploy rather than by a manual pass.

One test in tests/test_settlement_classes.py, and it pins the taxonomy against
the published evidence rather than against a guess.
