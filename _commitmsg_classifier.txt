Publish the settlement classifier, and close two of the three PayAI follow-ups

ITEM 1 — DOES CDP CHARGE US? No, and it is measured. CDP gives 1,000 free
onchain transactions a month. Our own settlements: 4 in September, 26 in
October, 60 in November to date. That is 0.4–6% of the free tier, and rejected
attempts cost nothing because verification is free. Our facilitator cost is $0.
Not moving to PayAI for Base is now a measured decision, not a preference:
$0.00179 a settlement against a $0.003 sale is 60% of the transaction.

ITEM 3 — SOLANA AS A SECOND accepts[] NETWORK IS BLOCKED, and not by
configuration. There is no Solana wallet and no Solana configuration anywhere:
zero mentions of Solana in the codebase bar one test fixture, and none of the 40
Render env vars names one. Quoting a Solana payTo we do not control would be
advertising an address the money goes to and nobody can collect, and our whole
settlement rail verifies Base EIP-3009 through the CDP facilitator — it cannot
verify an SPL transfer at all. An accept we cannot verify is worse than no
accept, so this needs a wallet, a custody decision and a verification path
first. Reported rather than half-built.

ITEM 4 — GET /api/v1/settlement-classes, free. This is PayAI's ask of
05.11.2026 answered for our own ledger: "label every settlement as agent, script
or noise, then publish the clean number". From onchain_sales in production
(113 rows, $0.997):

    third-party paying addresses.

Free on purpose and added to X402_FREE_ENDPOINTS: a statement nobody can read
without paying is not a statement. The classes come from on-chain behaviour
(fan-out fingerprinting, self-payment) plus our own key's identity — already
persisted per sale as onchain_sales.payer_class — so a payer cannot relabel
itself by changing its User-Agent. The route also states what it is NOT: our
ledger, not the ecosystem's, and a worked example of the method rather than a
market measurement.

hold the route to the honesty: that it stays free with the tier exhausted, and
