# Offer and Ad Strategy

## Product claim
**Получавай живи крипто анализи, DeFi сигнали и VIP Telegram достъп.**

## Target audience
- retail crypto traders;
- DeFi participants;
- market-followers who need signals;
- traders who want a simpler decision layer.

## Core offer
**VIP crypto intelligence** — Telegram + AI market bulletin + DeFi evaluation + premium access.

## Offer structure
### 1. Starter
- weekly bulletin
- Telegram alerts
- basic access

### 2. Pro
- daily insights
- premium analysis
- VIP channel access

### 3. API / Data access
- paid access to market intelligence endpoints
- wallet-based / x402 pricing
- business or power-user segment

## Creative angles
1. "AI market signals for crypto traders"
2. "Real-time DeFi intelligence"
3. "Telegram VIP access for active market followers"
4. "Smarter decisions without noise"

## Recommended channels
- Meta Ads
- Google Search / YouTube SEO
- X (Twitter)
- Telegram communities
- short-form video content

## Copy guidance
Не продавай "AI system". Продай резултат:
- по-добро вземане на решения;
- по-ясна картина на пазара;
- по-малко шум;
- по-бърз доступ до важни сигнали.

## CTA examples
- Join VIP access
- Unlock market intelligence
- Get daily crypto signals
- Start with the Pro plan

## Launch principle
Ограничаване на обхвата: 1 offer + 1 channel + 1 audience + 1 CTA.
