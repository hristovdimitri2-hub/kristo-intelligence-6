Let the whale feed's caller ask for fewer rows, and keep the test count true

The whale feed was the one paid route whose response could cost the buyer more
in model tokens than the call costs in USDC: the 50-row default is ~16.9 KB /
~4.2k tokens, against $0.003. A caller on a mid model pays ~$0.012 for those
tokens (4x the price) and on a frontier model ~$0.061 (20x). The handler read
its limit from WHALEFLOW_LIMIT, so the caller could not ask for less.

main.py
  * /api/v1/whaleflow now reads ?limit= (1..200) and ?hours= (1..168) from
    request.args, falling back to the existing env defaults. The caller may
    lower the response, never raise it past the ceiling the env already
    enforced; a non-numeric value falls back instead of failing a paid call.
  * Both knobs are declared in X402_INPUT_QUERY_PARAMS, so the 402 tells the
    buyer they exist. The alias /api/v1/whale-alerts inherits them by the
    existing alias loop.

tests/test_bazaar_output_example.py
  * test_input_declaration_is_unchanged keeps its teeth for the routes that
    still read nothing (/api/arb/opportunities, /api/sales) and no longer
    asserts the empty map for whaleflow.
  * test_whaleflow_declares_the_two_token_levers pins the declaration on both
    the canonical route and the alias, and checks the published example is the
    small one.
