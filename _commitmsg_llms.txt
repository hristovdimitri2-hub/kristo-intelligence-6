llms.txt: advertise the two new free routes

Both shipped in the last two commits and neither was discoverable from the file
an agent reads first. `/api/v1/settlement-classes` and
`/.well-known/paycheck.json` now appear under "Free (always accessible)", with
what each is for rather than just its path.

The classifier entry says the unflattering part out loud — most of our settled
volume is our own money — because that is the reason to publish it at all, and a
description that hides it would undercut the route it describes.

Caught before deploy: `llms_txt` is built with `str.format`, so the literal
`{"payTo": ...}` in the new text was read as a format specifier and the whole
file raised `ValueError: Invalid format specifier ' [...]'`. Escaped to `{{`.
The failure took out ten tests across four files, which is the useful shape for
a template bug — it fails loudly rather than emitting a broken file.
