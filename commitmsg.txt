fix(monitor): retry wallet init forever instead of exiting when RPC is down

Live production logs (2026-08-24) showed the blockchain monitor thread
permanently exiting when the public Base RPC (mainnet.base.org) was
unreachable at startup: "Blockchain monitor: no wallet - thread exiting."
That meant payment detection would NEVER start, even after the RPC
recovered.

Fix: the monitor now retries wallet initialization indefinitely
(BLOCKCHAIN_RETRY_INTERVAL, default 120s, min 60s). As soon as the RPC
is reachable it connects, and incoming USDC transfers to the fee
receiver are detected as before. Concept unchanged.

pytest: 45/45 PASS.
