Harden _adopt_already_settled: the body is a claim, not evidence

A facilitator's 400 body can name the transaction for an authorization that is
already on-chain, and we adopted it as a settled payment. The body is a CLAIM:
nothing in it proves that hash moved the buyer's USDC to us.

Two bounds now apply. The value must at least LOOK like a transaction hash
(0x + 64 hex, and hex-parseable) — before this, any string in that field, a URL
or a label or a number, was adopted and passed on as a settlement. And the
caller reads that receipt and verifies a USDC Transfer to our receiver for at
least the price before delivering (the check added in d0f7936), so an unrelated
or invented hash fails there instead of unlocking a free call.

Test: test_an_adopted_already_settled_tx_must_be_a_hash covers the good hash,
six malformed shapes, and the "already" gate.
