FIXED: /api/connectors CLAIMED gas=True

_eip3009_detail printed `gas={bool(os.getenv("WALLET_PRIVATE_KEY"))}` — which
says only that a KEY EXISTS. The one reader who cares about that line is an
operator during exactly such an outage, and `gas=True` told them the opposite of
the truth.

`_gas_wallet_status()` now returns absent | key-set | funded | empty | unknown:

  * the PUBLIC line (free, unauthenticated) says `key-set, funds unprobed` and
    claims NOTHING about funds — probing there would cost an RPC per anonymous
    request for a nicer word;
  * the OPERATOR view actually probes and says `funded` or
    `EMPTY — self-broadcast cannot pay`. The probe is cached (600s funded, 60s
    otherwise) and a failed probe reports `unknown` instead of guessing.

The balance read lives in `_gas_balance_wei` so the tests can cover the empty,
funded and failed branches without a network.
