Add /.well-known/paycheck.json, and record the two new measurement layers

WHAT WAS ADDED
GET /.well-known/paycheck.json returns {"payTo": ["0xd4cd…d08f"]}, the shape
Lumiere PayCheck documents. It reads X402_RECEIVER_ADDRESS rather than repeating
the address, because a declaration that can disagree with the 402 quote it
describes is worse than no declaration — and a test asserts the two are the same
fact. Free (added to X402_FREE_ENDPOINTS): a wallet rotation has to be
recognisable exactly when traffic is heaviest, not only below the free tier.

Why it matters: Lumiere watches the payTo of every endpoint it lists. A change it
cannot confirm is "avoid" for 24 hours, then "caution". This file is what it
cross-checks against.

Four tests in tests/test_paycheck_declaration.py: the documented shape, the
declared-wallet-equals-quoted-wallet invariant, free with the tier exhausted, and
EIP-55 checksumming.

HANDOVER section 30 records what the investigation found, and two of the findings
are corrections rather than news:

* Settled's scout has paid us 3 times (0xA1d1C0cf…, $0.009, 14.10 and 30.10) and
  all 3 delivered — matching the rows already in our own payment_guards. We score
  85/79/73 with delivery 15/15 and a "pay" recommendation.
* Its `latency 1/10` on /api/v1/signal is NOT a code defect and must not be
  "fixed": the 402 answers in 330–840 ms when measured live, and the 2123 ms
  average is a 30-day window polluted by 4 failed probes out of 29. It decays.
* x402-trust has us as providerVerified with host grade B (78.68) and 1×A + 11×B
  on the paid rows — and the dead Vercel copy as 18 rows, 14 of them grade F.
  Unlike x402scan, x402-trust supports self-service removal with the provider
  key we already hold. NOT executed: removal is irreversible.
