WHAT WE NEVER MEASURED

nohumans.directory publishes "94% of settled x402 calls delivered" — for OTHER
sellers. We had never computed ours. Measured it from production
(_forensics/our_deliver_ratio_guards.py):

    payment_guards: 94 rows, 86 with delivered_at  ->  91.5%
    settled-not-delivered: 8

payment_attempts has NO delivered_at, so the split can only come from
payment_guards — the durable claim ledger, written at settlement and stamped by
the after_request hook when a response actually goes out.

Of the eight: FIVE are our own demo wallet (0xe50c5212…), two are canaries or
crawlers (chet_payapi_verification, market_crawler_a19f), and ONE IS A REAL
EXTERNAL PAYER.

THE REAL PAYER: $0.003 AND A 405

0x0bbd2fec…, 2026-10-21, /api/v1/signal, classed `external`, never retried.
Traced to the second in request_log:

    17:51:39.608  POST /api/v1/signal -> 402   JarvisClaw-Federation/1.0
    17:51:44.064  payment_guards: consumed                     <- money taken
    17:51:44.071  POST /api/v1/signal -> 405   JarvisClaw-Federation/1.0  <- nothing

He sent POST to a GET route. before_request runs even when the URL fails to
match by method, so the paywall settled the payment and the dispatcher then
answered 405. Exactly what r/x402 warns about: "validate before settlement, so a
bad request never burns a payment signature".

THIS ONE IS ALREADY FIXED (main.py:5352-5396 — the guard runs before any
credential is read, with `Allow` and payment_taken: false), and verified LIVE:

    POST /api/v1/signal -> 405  Allow=GET, HEAD, OPTIONS
      {"error":"method_not_allowed", ... "payment_taken": false}

with test_a_wrong_method_never_reaches_the_settlement_rail proving the settlement
rail is never reached. So the finding is not a new defect — it is the real-money
confirmation of why that fix exists.
